VJIT Fee Structure (2024-2025):
1. B.Tech Tuition Fee: ₹1,15,000 per year.
2. M.Tech Tuition Fee: ₹57,000 per year.
3. MBA Tuition Fee: ₹51,800 per year.
4. Admission Fee: ₹5,500 (One time).
5. NBA Accreditation Fee: ₹3,000 per year (for accredited branches).
6. Transport Fee: Ranges from ₹20,000 to ₹35,000 depending on distance.
7. Exam Fee: ₹1200 per semester (Regular).
