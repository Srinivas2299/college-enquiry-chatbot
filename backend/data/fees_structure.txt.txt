VJIT Fee Structure (2024-2025):

This fee structure applies to all B.Tech branches, including CSE, AI & ML (AIML), ECE, EEE, IT, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering, unless otherwise specified.

1. B.Tech Tuition Fee (all branches, including AI & ML / AIML): Rs.1,15,000 per year.
2. M.Tech Tuition Fee: Rs.57,000 per year.
3. MBA Tuition Fee: Rs.51,800 per year.
4. Admission Fee: Rs.5,500 (One time).
5. NBA Accreditation Fee: Rs.3,000 per year (for accredited branches).
6. Transport Fee: Ranges from Rs.20,000 to Rs.35,000 depending on distance.
7. Exam Fee: Rs.1200 per semester (Regular).

Note: These are standard B.Tech tuition fees and apply equally to the AI & ML (AIML) branch as they do to all other engineering branches at VJIT.
